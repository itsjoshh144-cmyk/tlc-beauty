# Outreach email templates

Replace everything in [brackets]. Keep each email short and personal: mention one real thing about their business.

> **UK rules (PECR/GDPR):** cold email is fine to limited companies and LLPs, as long as you say who you are and include an easy opt-out. Sole traders and partnerships count as individuals, so you need their consent before sending marketing emails. For those, a phone call, a DM or a visit in person is safer. Keep a list of anyone who opts out and never email them again.
